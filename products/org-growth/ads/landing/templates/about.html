About

From the slop, structure will emerge. 216Labs is a shipping lab: one monorepo, one deploy, and a bias to production. We take paid work — production web apps, AI-feature retainers, and specialist audio/ML pilots — and we open-source the shell that keeps vibe-coded codebases alive.

What we do
Contract delivery and retainers for teams that need something in production this month, plus audio/ML consulting on CARFAC, hydrophone, drone, and bird vocal work. Consumer experiments stay online; the ones that earn their keep stay in the hot pool.
How we work
Fast iteration, visible progress, and a single source of truth. When something is broken, we fix it and deploy. When a funnel works, we improve it and measure.
